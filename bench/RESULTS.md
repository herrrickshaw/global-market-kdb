# Benchmark: q vs DuckDB vs Polars on the study's own operations

Machine: arm64 27.2, Apple M1, 8 cores, 8 GB. KDB-X 5 2026.10.23 (Community, `-s 4`), DuckDB 2.0.0, Polars 2.1.0.

Median (min) wall time in ms over 5 runs, data in memory except for the load row. Generated by `bench/run.py`.

The machine has 8 GB and was swapping during these runs (other applications open), so timings are noisy: q's rolling-window step measured anywhere from 1.0 s to 9.6 s across separate runs. Read the minimum as the better estimate of each engine's speed, and differences under about 2x as noise.

| operation | q | q (peach / sorted) | duckdb-1t | duckdb-4t | polars-1t | polars-4t | check agrees |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| load US Parquet (16.7M rows) | 1,305 (1,290) | 1,240 (1,171) | 1,794 (1,173) | 368 (324) | 469 (379) | 303 (288) | yes |
| median $vol by sym, year | 1,791 (1,747) | 819 (746) | 1,125 (834) | 246 (237) | 1,549 (1,432) | 995 (975) | yes |
| 5d return + 63-row mean |r| by sym | 863 (841) | – | 9,708 (8,156) | 4,105 (3,022) | 882 (837) | 608 (552) | yes |
| rank 5d return by date | 2,872 (2,326) | 2,396 (1,857) | 29,475 (11,394) | 14,308 (5,224) | 1,063 (934) | 768 (714) | yes |
| as-of join IN bars to fundamentals | 78 (67) | – | 1,014 (948) | 588 (538) | 145 (138) | 75 (70) | yes |

Notes:
- `q` is a direct translation of each operation. `q (peach / sorted)` is the idiomatic version: `peach` over year files or years (KDB-X Community allows 4 secondary threads), and per-date ranking on rows stored by date, as they are in a date-partitioned HDB.
- q-sql on an in-memory table runs single-threaded unless the work is split with `peach`; DuckDB and Polars parallelise internally, so they are shown at 1 and 4 threads.
- The rank row uses ordinal ranks in all engines (q `rank`, DuckDB `row_number`, Polars `rank("ordinal")`); ties and nulls may be ordered differently, so only the row count is checked.
- This measures one laptop and one dataset. It is not a STAC-M3 style benchmark and makes no claim beyond these numbers.
